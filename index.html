Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

YQVENT ("we", "us") makes air distribution and ventilation products and runs the website yqvent.com. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website or contact us, what we do with it, and how you can ask us to delete it.

Information you give us

Enquiry form. When you send an enquiry through our website, we receive what you enter: your name, email address, product interest, quantity, destination and message, together with any files you attach, such as drawings or product lists. Fields marked * are needed so that we can reply. Each enquiry is given a reference number.

WhatsApp. If you message us on WhatsApp, whether from our website or from one of our advertisements, we receive your phone number, your WhatsApp profile name, and the messages and files you send. WhatsApp is operated by Meta, and its own privacy policy applies to your use of it.

Email and phone. If you email or call us, we receive your email address or phone number and whatever you choose to tell us.

Social media. If you comment on or message our pages on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube or TikTok, we see your profile name and the content of your comment or message, as shown to us by that platform.

Information collected automatically

Server logs. Like most websites, our web server records each request it receives: the IP address, the date and time, the page requested, the referring page, and the browser and device type. We use these logs to keep the website secure, to limit automated form submissions, to fix faults and to count visits.

Storage in your browser. To make the website work, it keeps a few items in your browser's own storage: your language choice, the products in your enquiry list, form drafts you have not yet sent, and the reference number of an enquiry you have sent. These stay on your device. We receive them only when you submit an enquiry. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

Our website does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics.

This policy page. This page is hosted for us by GitHub, separately from our website. GitHub logs the IP address of visitors to this page for security purposes.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare quotations and discuss your project.
  • To process and ship orders and to provide after-sales support.
  • To keep records of our business dealings with you.
  • To keep our website secure and working.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to make automated decisions about you.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom: we handle enquiry and order information because you asked us to, in order to enter into or carry out a contract with you, and we keep server logs because we have a legitimate interest in running a secure website.

Who can see your information

  • Our own sales and engineering staff.
  • Companies that host our systems or carry our communications: our hosting provider, our email provider and WhatsApp.
  • Carriers, freight forwarders and customs brokers, when this is needed to ship an order to you.
  • Public authorities, when the law requires it.

Our staff work in China and our website server is located in Singapore, so your information is processed outside your own country.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and correspondence: for as long as we are dealing with your request or doing business with you, and for up to three years after our last contact.
  • Order records: for as long as accounting and tax law requires.
  • Server logs: for no longer than 90 days.

Our use of the Meta platform

We use an internal tool, YQVENT Workbench, that connects to Meta's developer platform. Our staff use it to publish our own videos and posts to our own Facebook Page and Instagram account, and to prepare advertisements in our own advertising account. It is not available to the public, it does not offer Facebook Login, and it does not collect, store or share personal information about Facebook or Instagram users.

How to delete your data

You can ask us at any time to delete the personal information we hold about you.

  1. Send an email to huangjiayi498@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Tell us the email address or phone number you used to contact us and, if you have one, your enquiry reference number.
  3. We will delete your enquiries, attachments, emails and WhatsApp conversations from our systems within 30 days and confirm to you by email.

We may keep records that the law requires us to keep, such as invoices for completed orders.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To use any of these rights, email us at the address above. We reply within 30 days.

Children

Our website is intended for business buyers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
